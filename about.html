Who We Are

DoBu Martial Arts is a professional martial arts training centre offering world-class instruction in Karate, Judo, Jiu-Jitsu and Muay Thai. We also provide fitness training and self-defence courses for people of all ages.

Our experienced instructors are committed to helping students improve their physical fitness, confidence, discipline and self-defence skills in a safe and friendly environment.

🥋 Karate 🥋 Judo 🥋 Jiu-Jitsu 🥊 Muay Thai

Our Mission

To provide high-quality martial arts training that inspires confidence, respect and lifelong learning.

Our Vision

To become one of the leading martial arts training centres by providing professional coaching and excellent customer service.
